# Wrap-up and Next Steps

**Status:** Draft

> Written at the end of a phase, term or project. It is what the next team (or future you) reads first. Lines starting with "Guide:" explain what to write.

## 1. Outcome Snapshot
Guide: what was delivered, what was not, and the final state of the gates.

## 2. Keep, Cut, Borrow
- **Keep:** Guide: practices and tools that worked.
- **Cut:** Guide: what wasted time.
- **Borrow:** Guide: ideas to take to the next project.

## 3. Learnings (blameless)
Guide: three to five lessons, each with what happened and what to do differently.

## 4. Continuation
Guide: who owns the system now, where it runs, how to hand it over (accounts, documents, data).

## 5. Final Readiness Check
- [ ] Deployed version matches the documented version.
- [ ] Accounts and secrets handed over safely, or revoked.
- [ ] Backups verified.
- [ ] Open issues listed in `docs/deferred-features.md`.
