# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

**Status:** Draft

> Why the project matters to the organization or business. The PRD says what to build; the BRD says why it is worth building. Lines starting with "Guide:" explain what to write.

## 1. Executive Summary
Guide: one paragraph a busy decision-maker can read.

## 2. Problem and Opportunity
Guide: the problem, who pays for it today (time, money, risk), and the opportunity if it is solved.

## 3. Business Model
Guide: who pays, for what, and how; or for internal and academic projects, who funds and sustains it.
